How We Build Trust & Safety

Your safety and security are our top priorities. Every user goes through our comprehensive verification process.

Identity Verification

All users must verify their identity with government-issued ID documents. We use secure, encrypted technology to verify authenticity.

Required for All Users

Business Verification

Operators must provide proof of business registration. We verify with Companies House and check their operational history.

For Operators

Property Verification

Property owners must provide proof of ownership or authorization to lease. We verify title deeds and landlord documentation.

For Property Owners

Insurance & Compliance

Operators must maintain proper insurance and comply with all local STR regulations. We verify certificates and licenses.

For Operators

Background Checks

We conduct background checks on all operators, including credit checks and criminal record verification for your peace of mind.

For Operators

Review System

Our transparent review system lets landlords and operators rate each other, building a community of trusted partners.
